- 2026-08-28 08:11–08:35 CST / branch: repair/full-audit
  - Focus: /loop iteration 2 — spread mode (series A minus series B).
  - Added:
    - `SeriesAnalytics.spread(_:minus:)` and a `ChartMode` of Overlay / Spread (A − B).
      Charts a yield curve (DGS10 − DGS2), a real rate (DGS10 − T10YIE), an unemployment
      gap (UNRATE − NROU), or a credit spread (BAA − AAA) directly.
    - A `DisplayedSeries` layer between windowing and rendering. The chart, statistics,
      data table, hover readout, recession band extents, and exports now all read from it,
      so a spread cannot show one thing on the chart and another in the table.
    - `ValueFormatter.representsDifference`: a spread of two rates is measured in
      percentage points, and every surface labels it that way.
    - Spread mode is offered only when the units are comparable, states why when it is not,
      and falls back to Overlay when the series it was differencing against is removed.
  - Mistake caught by a test, and the design change it forced:
    - The first implementation matched the subtrahend by *nearest date*. The mixed-cadence
      test then returned 5 points where 6 were expected: with a monthly minuend and a
      quarterly subtrahend, the third month of each quarter sits 61 days from the quarter
      start and fell outside the tolerance. Nearest-date matching therefore punches a
      silent hole in the last month of every quarter.
    - Replaced with last-observation-carried-forward bounded by one subtrahend period,
      which is the standard treatment: a quarterly reading describes its whole quarter.
      The bound stops a series that has stopped publishing from being differenced against
      indefinitely, and points predating the subtrahend are dropped, never extrapolated.
  - Verification:
    - `xcodebuild ... build` -> BUILD SUCCEEDED, zero warnings.
    - `xcodebuild ... -only-testing:FRED-UltraTests test` -> TEST SUCCEEDED, 109 tests
      (9 new, including the carry-forward window, the backward-extrapolation guard, and
      the full spread-mode surface).

- 2026-08-28 08:45–23:20 CST / branch: repair/full-audit
  - Focus: /loop iteration 3 — the two things the user named: unit parsing/normalization,
    and a schema for related data series.
  - Method note: before writing the parser I sampled ~350 real FRED series and collected
    the 43 distinct unit strings they actually publish. That sample, not guesswork, is
    what the parser and its tests are built against.
  - Live bugs the sample exposed:
    - `Dollars per Million BTU` and `U.S. Dollars per Million Metric British Thermal Unit`
      picked up "million" from the *denominator* as a scale, so $3.50/MMBTU rendered as
      "$3.5M". Two more forms hit the same path.
    - `Dollars per Hour` (37 of the sampled series) was canonicalised to "U.S. Dollars" and
      therefore judged interchangeable with `Billions of Dollars` — a $28 wage would share
      an axis with $29T of GDP and the app would call the units compatible.
    - `2010 U.S. Dollars` and `1982-84 CPI Adjusted Dollars` were treated as nominal, so
      constant-dollar and current-dollar series could be plotted together.
    - A bare `Thousands` was scaled by 1,000 while still being labelled "Thousands".
  - Unit work:
    - Split every unit string on its first " per " and take family and magnitude from the
      numerator only; the denominator is retained and must match for two units to be
      comparable.
    - New `MagnitudeParser` handling word chains ("Hundreds of Millions" = 1e8), explicit
      factors ("0.4 Billion", "100 Billions"), and bare literals ("1,000,000",
      "1,000,000s"), while never reading a four-digit year or an index base period as a
      multiplier.
    - Basis points normalise onto the percent base (25bp charts as 0.25%), with the
      published figure preserved in the table and exports.
    - New `.ratio` family so a ratio of 0.0234 is not rounded to 0.02.
  - Mistake caught by a test: period-stripping for abbreviations ("U.S." -> "us") was also
    eating decimal points, so "0.4 Billion" parsed as 4 billion — an order of magnitude
    out. Periods between digits are now preserved, mirroring the digit-group comma rule.
  - Related-series work:
    - Confirmed the shapes of `series/categories`, `series/release`, `series/tags`, and
      `category/series` against the live API first.
    - New schema: `FREDCategory`, `FREDRelease`, `FREDTag` (with group labels), and
      `SeriesRelations`.
    - `FREDService.relations(for:)` fetches the three context calls concurrently, then
      pulls category siblings by popularity. Each part degrades independently, and the
      whole result is memoised.
    - The Insights tab shows category, a linked release, descriptive tags (licence and
      citation tags filtered out), and sibling series ranked so unit-comparable ones come
      first, each with a one-click Compare.
    - Loading is lazy: four extra requests are not spent for a reader who never opens
      Insights.

    - `xcodebuild ... -only-testing:FRED-UltraTests test` -> TEST SUCCEEDED, 124 tests
